S4W Flood Observation Submission

Use this form to document a flood event in Nepal. You can preview your information before submitting it for S4W staff review.

Submitted observations are kept private until reviewed. Only verified observations approved by authorized S4W staff may be published on the public flood map. Photograph uploads are not available yet.

Click the map to select the location where the flood was observed. The latitude and longitude fields below will fill automatically.

Selected Photograph Preview

Submitting an observation does not make it public. S4W staff must review and approve the information before it can appear on the flood map. Photograph permission is handled separately.

Check this box if you select a photograph. Photographs are currently previewed only and are not uploaded with the observation. S4W staff will review photograph permissions before any photograph is published.

Previewing does not save or upload your observation. Clicking "Submit Observation for S4W Review" sends the observation for private staff review once submissions are enabled. Nothing appears on the public flood map until authorized S4W staff approve it.
