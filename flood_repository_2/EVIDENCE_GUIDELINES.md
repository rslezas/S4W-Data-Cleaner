## Evidence Review: 
 Before an evidence item is published the reviewer should record its original URL, confirm the reported data and location, check whether the evidence corresponds to the flood, record the author and permissions, compare claims with independent documentation, assign a verification status and record the reviewer and review date. 
## Definitions: 
1. Reviewed: examined for completeness and plausibiity but not independently confirmed 
2. Verified: Supported by reliable evidence sufficient to substantiate the specific claim. 
3. Documented by official source: An official publication documents the event. Not automatically verifying photographs, coordinates or boundaries. 
4. Demo: Fictional test record, never to be represented as an actual flood. 
## Accuracy Rules: 
Identify whether coordinates represent a verified observation, an approximate location, or a general reference point. Do not label approximate locations as verified flood sites. do not present circles or polygons as observed flood extens. Lastly, record uncertainty when a location cannot be independently confirmed. 
## Photograph and video permissions: 
Before publishing any media make sure that the project has permission to display or redistribute it. Record the creator and applicable rights or license. Obtain consent for materials. Check for faces, private information or sensitive locations. Blur or exclude sensitive details. Also link to the original source rather than hosting a copy. 
## Licensing:
 Make sure to confirm the applicable license for each dataset, attribute government and research sources appropriately, retain required OpenStreetMap attribution and comply with OpenStreetMap requirements. Lastly, do not assume that all website content can be distributed with the same license. 
## Corrections: 
If someone reports inaccurate informaton you must record the request and affected item, review the information, restrict or remove during review, correct or remove the record if need be and document the decision and update the repository. 
## Final Publication Checklist
 Before publishing a flood event or evidence item make sure the event date, location, and description are accurate and supported by the cited source. Make sure the original evidence source and URL are recored, the verification status is correctly assigned. Make sure the reviewer and review date are documented, geographic coordinates and any uncertainty are clearly identified. Make sure that photographs and videos have appropriate permissions and attribution. Importantly ensure that personal and sensitive nformation has been removed or protected, demonstration records are clearly labeled as fictional and that the published information has been checked for accuracy and consistency.
