Purpose: develop an interactive, event based website that organizes and presents documented historical flood events in the Kathmandu Valley. The repository will combine reliable historical sources with relevant S4W-Nepal hydrological monitoring data to make flood information more accessible. 
Users: S4W-Nepal researchers and staff, students and academic researchers, Kathmandu valley residents, and other organizations studying flooding and water resources. 
Initial Flood Event: The repo's first documented event is the September 27th-29th, 2024 Kathmandu Valley flood, using a situational report from Nepal's Department of Hydrology and Meteorology. 
Evidence Sources: The repository may incorporate government reports and official flood documentation, existing S4W-Nepal monitoring data,  photographs and videos with approporiate permissions from previous flood events, as well as credible news reports and other documented sources.
Verification: Records will idenfity their sources and verification status. Demonstration data will remain clearly labeled and separate from documented events. Flood locations and boundaries will not be presented as verified unless supported by appropriate geographic evidence. 
Website features: The current prototype includes an interactive OpenStreet/Leaflet map, event timeline location and evidence filters, downloadable records and source documentation. 
Future Development: Expanding the historical floods database, adding relevant rainfall and other monitoring data from S4W-Nepal and establish a repeatable process for publishing new events. 
Publication: The website will be maintained through version-controlled project files. Public hosting, licensing and evidence permissions and procedurres for corrections will be finalized with S4W-Nepal before publication.
