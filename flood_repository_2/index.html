About This Project

Devastating flood events cause loss of life and damage to property and infrastructure every year in the Kathmandu Valley. This tool collects photographic and video observations from impacted communities to help fellow Valley residents understand their flood risk. The flood tracker is brought to you by Smartphones for Water-Nepal, an organization that empowers the community to protect themselves against water-related disasters by collecting localized water data through images and videos.
